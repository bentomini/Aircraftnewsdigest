**FAA AD 2026-13-06: Trent XWB-84/-97 fuel manifold hose inspection/replacement — A350-900/-1000 fleet (effective July 17)** — A350-900, A350-1000, 2026-07-17.

- *What happened:* FAA issued AD 2026-13-06 (FR doc 2026-13481, Amendment 39-23388; published July 2, effective July 17, 2026), superseding AD 2024-25-10 and expanding applicability to include Trent XWB-75/-79/-79B/-84 in addition to XWB-97. **EASA AD 2026-0134: A321neo ELAC B L104 solar-radiation vulnerability — supersedes EAD 2025-0268-E (effective July 22)** — A321neo, 2026-07-22.

- *What happened:* EASA issued AD 2026-0134 (effective July 22, 2026) superseding Emergency AD 2025-0268-E for certain Airbus A320-family aircraft including A321neo variants. The AD requires replacement of ELAC B L104 computers with the ELAC B L103+ standard per Airbus SBs A320-27-1315/1316/1317 or equivalent approved instructions. High-energy solar particles cause single-event upsets (bit-flips) in the ELAC B L104 microprocessor, corrupting flight-control data and creating uncommanded flight-control inputs. The original EAD was issued November 28, 2025 following the JetBlue A320 uncommanded pitch-down event (October 30, 2025).
- *Technical detail:* EASA AD 2026-0134 (effective 2026-07-22); effectivity: certain Airbus A319, A320, and A321 CEO and NEO variants; A321neo sub-variants stated as included per multiple secondary sources — specific sub-variant list requires primary-source confirmation. [UNVERIFIED — reference exists, text not accessed] EAD 2025-0268-E (EASA, 2025-11-28); A320 family — superseded. [UNVERIFIED — reference exists, text not accessed] Regulator position: ELAC B L103+ standard installation required per Airbus SBs A320-27-1315/1316/1317; ELAC B L104 standard prohibited for installation.

- *What happened:* FAA issued an AD (FR doc 2026-11511; published June 9, effective July 14, 2026) adopting EASA AD 2025-0209 for certain Airbus A350-941 aircraft. - *Technical detail:* AD 2026-11511 (published 2026-06-09, effective 2026-07-14); effectivity: certain Airbus A350-941 per EASA AD 2025-0209 (claimed MSN 0005–0047; not confirmed without primary-source access). Note: this is a separate and distinct defect from the missing-sealant action (EASA AD 2025-0210) below.
