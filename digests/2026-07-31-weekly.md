**A330-200/-300 (STC-modified) flightcrew oxygen AD limits ETOPS-180 to three crew** — A330-300, 2026-07-30.
*What happened:* FAA adopted AD 2026-15-01 for Airbus A330-200/-300 aircraft modified by STC ST04038NY, after finding the single-cylinder flightcrew oxygen supply insufficient for ETOPS-180 with four flightcrew members; requires an AFM Supplement revision limiting ETOPS-180 to three flightcrew and correcting minimum dispatch pressure data.
*Technical detail:* AD 2026-15-01; Amendment 39-23413; Docket FAA-2026-3873; Project MCAI-2025-00197-T, Airbus A330-200 and A330-300 series airplanes modified in accordance with FAA STC ST04038NY, certificated in any category. The AD text does not give an MSN-specific list; applies to all airplanes carrying this STC. Single-cylinder flightcrew oxygen supply insufficient for ETOPS-180 with four flightcrew members. [VERIFIED — primary source] ([source](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/full_text/html/2026/07/30/2026-15455.html))
*Verbatim:* "limit ETOPS-180 operations to three flightcrew members on airplanes with a flightcrew oxygen system supplied by a single oxygen cylinder" — Airworthiness Directives; Airbus SAS Airplanes (AD 2026-15-01), AD 2026-15-01, Amendment 39-23413; July 30, 2026, [link](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/full_text/html/2026/07/30/2026-15455.html).

**K2 Airways 737-400 freighter lost in Arabian Sea after reported nav-system malfunction, all 5 crew missing** — Boeing 737-400 (freighter), 2026-07-07.
*What happened:* K2 Airways Flight 1732, a 27-year-old Boeing 737-4M0(BDSF) freighter (2x CFM56-3C1) flying Sharjah-Karachi, crashed into the Arabian Sea on 7 July 2026 after the crew reported a navigation-system malfunction, followed by erratic altitude excursions (a 5,600ft descent, 7,200ft climb within 30 seconds) and a final steep descent from 36,550ft.
