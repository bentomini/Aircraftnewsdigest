## Directly Fleet-Relevant

**FAA AD 2025-25-12: A350 flight-control software update closes out FCRM hydraulic-fluid contamination risk (effective Jan 13, 2026) [Carried-over developing event]** — A350-900, A350-1000, 2025-12-29.
*What happened:* FAA AD 2025-25-12 (Amendment 39-23220, docket FAA-2025-5390), published 29 December 2025 and effective 13 January 2026, requires Airbus A350-941/-1041 operators to install Flight Control and Guidance System software standards PRIM P14.1.3 and SEC S14.1.2, permanently closing out a risk of loss of control of an outboard aileron (and, by shared design, elevator/rudder) caused by hydraulic fluid contaminating an electronic card in the Flight Control Remote Module. It supersedes interim AD 2025-13-12 (July 2025).
*Technical detail:* FAA AD 2025-25-12, Airbus SAS Model A350-941 and A350-1041 airplanes [VERIFIED — primary source] ([source](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2025-12-29/html/2025-23858.htm)); software fix positioned as the permanent mitigation, replacing the interim hardware-replacement program required by superseded AD 2025-13-12; hydraulic fluid contaminated an electronic card of the Flight Control Remote Module (FCRM), which could cause loss of control of an outboard aileron surface.
*Verbatim:* "Loss of control of an outboard aileron surface due to hydraulic fluid contaminating an electronic card of the flight control remote module." — Airworthiness Directives; Airbus SAS Airplanes, FAA AD 2025-25-12, 2025-12-29, [link](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2025-12-29/html/2025-23858.htm).

**FAA AD 2025-25-07: GE90 HPT stage 1/2 disk replacement for iron-inclusion powder-metal defect (effective Feb 6, 2026) [Carried-over developing event]** — 777-300ER, 2026-01-02.
*What happened:* FAA AD 2025-25-07 (docket FAA-2025-1364), published 2 January 2026 and effective 6 February 2026, requires replacement of GE90-90B/-94B/-110B1/-115B HPT stage 1 and stage 2 disks identified in AD Table 1 or referenced GE service-bulletin tables, due to a manufacturing process that may have introduced iron inclusion into the powder-metal material, risking premature fracture and uncontained failure. GE90-115B powers the CX 777-300ER fleet.
*Technical detail:* FAA AD 2025-25-07, General Electric GE90-90B, GE90-94B, GE90-110B1, and GE90-115B turbofan engines with specific HPT stage 1/2 disks identified by part/serial number [VERIFIED — primary source] ([source](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-01-02/html/2025-24173.htm)); HPT stage 1/2 disks manufactured from powder metal material suspected to contain iron inclusion, reducing fatigue life and raising risk of premature fracture / uncontained failure.
*Verbatim:* "The FAA is issuing this AD to prevent premature fracture and uncontained failure of the HPT stage 1 disks and HPT stage 2 disks." — Airworthiness Directives; General Electric Company Engines, FAA AD 2025-25-07, 2026-01-02, [link](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-01-02/html/2025-24173.htm).

**NTSB UPS MD-11 (Flight 2976, docket DCA26MA024) investigative hearing — May 19-20, 2026: aft-pylon bearing fatigue findings [Carried-over developing event]** — 2026-05-19.
*What happened:* NTSB held a two-day investigative hearing (19-20 May 2026, docket DCA26MA024, confirmed on ntsb.gov) into the 4 November 2025 crash of UPS Flight 2976, an MD-11F that lost its No.1 engine/pylon on takeoff from Louisville, killing 3 crew and 11 on the ground. Trade press reporting on the hearing describes fatigue cracking in an aft pylon spherical bearing (P/N S00399-1), prior bearing-failure incidents referenced in a 2011 Boeing service letter (MD-11-SL-54-104-A), and Boeing testimony that the bearing should have been classified as a Principal Structural Element at original certification. The FAA's emergency AD grounded MD-11/DC-10/MD-10 variants in November 2025; that grounding was eased in May 2026 per trade press.
*Technical detail:* NTSB docket DCA26MA024 — Investigative Hearing [VERIFIED — primary source] ([source](https://www.ntsb.gov/news/events/Pages/DCA26MA024InvHearing.aspx)); FAA emergency AD (MD-11/DC-10/MD-10 grounding, Nov 2025), MD-11, MD-11F, MD-10-10F, MD-10-30F, DC-10-10/-10F/-15/-30/-30F (incl. KC-10A/KDC-10), DC-10-40/-40F [UNVERIFIED — reference exists, text not accessed]; reported (trade press characterization of hearing testimony): Boeing staff acknowledged the failed aft pylon spherical bearing should have been classified as a Principal Structural Element during original MD-11 certification; reported (trade press characterization): fatigue cracking in the bearing race propagated until overstress fracture of both lugs, shifting load and allowing engine/pylon separation.
*Verbatim:* "The three crewmembers aboard the airplane were killed. Eleven people on the ground were killed and another 23 injured." — Investigative Hearing: UPS Flight 2976 Crash During Takeoff, DCA26MA024, 2026-05-19, [link](https://www.ntsb.gov/news/events/Pages/DCA26MA024InvHearing.aspx).
